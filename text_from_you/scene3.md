## Scene 3: The Great Silence (The Testing Ground)

### 1. The Asymmetric Void
* **The Interrogations:** Following the "I'm you" reveal, Lauren tries to use the thread as an oracle for difficult medical cases (`"Autoimmune or oncology?"`) and major career choices (`"Should I take Chief Resident?"`).
* **The Read Receipts:** Every text shows `Read` immediately, but Future Lauren refuses to answer.
* **The Psychological Trap:** Current Lauren feels tested, observed, and ignored by her older self.

### 2. Forced Autonomy & Real Life
* **Medical Mastery:** Without shortcuts from the future, Lauren is forced to rely entirely on her own intellect, research, and medical intuition, building true professional mastery.
* **Grounding in Reality:** During the silence, Greg becomes her anchor. With no future warnings to overcomplicate things, their comfortable, practical relationship quietly solidifies into a marriage.
* **The False Sense of Security:** As years pass without a single message, Lauren convinces herself the anomaly is over and that she is in total control of her destiny.
