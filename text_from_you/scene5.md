# Scene 5: The Final Sign-Off (1 New Message)

## 1. The Decades of Drift
* **The Amicable Split:** Just as predicted, years after the children are grown, Lauren and Greg quietly, respectfully part ways. There is no explosive drama—just two people who loved each other well, completed their shared journey, and moved on.
* **The Living:** Free from the fear of impending tragedy, Lauren lives fully. She excels in her career, stays present with her kids, and accepts the natural, quiet flow of time.
* **The Silent Phone:** The text thread sits dormant for over twenty-five years. It becomes an ancient, archived conversation on an old device—a strange, sacred memory from her youth.

## 2. The Hospice Room
* **Setting:** Decades later. An elderly Dr. Lauren Vance sits in a quiet, sunlit room in a care facility. Her body is fading, but her mind remains sharp and peaceful.
* **The Realization:** Looking down at her bedside table, she sees her phone. She realizes she has finally arrived at the other end of the bridge. She is no longer the anxious young doctor receiving the texts; she is the dying woman holding the phone.

## 3. The Final Loop
* **The Action:** With quiet, trembling hands, she opens the ancient thread with her own name at the top. She looks back through forty years of logged notes, panicked questions, and silent read receipts.
* **The Final Text:** She taps out her final message to the young, terrified version of herself sitting in that dim kitchen decades ago:
  > `"Hey, it's been great chatting with my past self. The doctors say it doesn't look good. Live each day like it's your last. Signing off now. Love you always, past self."`
* **The Send:** She hits send. On her screen, the badge updates one last time: **1 New Message**. She closes her eyes, sits back in the quiet, and lets go.

## 4. The Structural Resolution
* **The Loop Closes:** The mystery was never a temporal weapon, a dark portal, or a cosmic prank. It was simply an old woman reaching back through time to tell her younger self to stop worrying, trust her own strength, and savor the ride.
