# Scene 5: The Immutable Past (1 New Message)

## 1. The Realization of Time
* **The Setup:** Current Lauren believes she is on the precipice of deciding Greg's fate. She's staring at the exploit code, calculating the risks, and waiting for the thread to give her permission.
* **The Final Ping:** The dormant thread lights up with its final, brutal transmission from the hospice bed decades later.

## 2. The Reveal
* **The Text:** 
  > `"Stop waiting for a sign. Greg died ten years ago. You took Option B. You kept the coat, took the money, and let him go. I've spent forty years sitting in this clean room with my clean license, watching our kids miss their father. There is no alternative path. I just needed you to know what we did."`

## 3. The Collapse of the Timeline
* **The Epiphany:** The horror hits Current Lauren—and the audience. The timeline isn't branching; it's fixed. The choice wasn't in front of her; it was behind her. 
* **The Ending Note:** The story ends not with a grand sci-fi rewrite or a happy ending, but with a quiet, lingering look at a woman holding a cold phone in a dark room, forced to live with the weight of the life she chose.
