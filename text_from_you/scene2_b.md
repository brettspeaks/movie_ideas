## Scene 2: The Escalation & The Confrontation

### 1. The Nagging Phase (Anxiety Ramp)
* **Shift in Content:** The thread shifts from external life hacks to invasive personal judgment (`"Don't drink so much"`, `"Call your mom"`, `"Go to bed"`).
* **Paranoia:** Lauren suspects a stalker, a hacked iCloud account, or spyware. She changes passwords, wipes her phone, and buys new hardware—but the thread persists.
* **Psychological Impact:** The thread targets her real-world guilt (stress-drinking, neglecting family, burnout), making her feel monitored in her own skin.

### 2. The "Who Are You?" Climax
* **Setting:** Late night in Lauren's apartment. Exhausted, holding a glass of wine.
* **The Interrogation:** Lauren abandons self-notes and explicitly demands answers (`"Who are you? What do you want?"`).
* **The Reveal:** The thread responds directly: `"I'm you. Put the glass down."`

### 3. Tonal Contrast
* **Subverting the Sci-Fi Trope:** No dramatic score or glowing portals. The moment is kept grounded in mundane reality—the hum of the fridge, the microwave clock, and the glow of an everyday iPhone screen.
