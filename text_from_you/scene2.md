## Scene 2: The "He'll Do" Progression

### Character Dynamic: Lauren & Greg
* **Initial Vibe:** Practical, low-drama compatibility. Greg is a solid 7/10 match who respects her hours, makes decent coffee, and doesn't demand dramatic validation. Lauren's internal assessment: *"He'll do."*
* **The Rom-Com Subversion:** No slow-motion rain kisses or grand romantic speeches. Their relationship is built on shared calendar invites, takeout containers after 14-hour shifts, and comfortable silence.
* **The Shift:** The terrifying text thread transforms a casual, "he'll do" marriage into a high-stakes race against an imaginary clock, forcing Lauren to realize how deeply she cared all along.
