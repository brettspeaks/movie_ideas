## Scene 1: The Rothschild Note

### Setting & Mood
* **Location:** Lauren’s quiet, dimly lit apartment following a brutal 14-hour ER/medical-legal shift.
* **Atmosphere:** Deep exhaustion, brain fog, the quiet hum of the refrigerator, a half-empty glass of Pinot Noir on the nightstand.

### The Action
1. **The Brain Dump:** Before turning off the light, Lauren does her usual routine: opens her self-text thread (`Me (iPhone)`), taps out two mundane reminders (*"Call pharmacy at 8 AM," "Dry cleaning"*), sets her phone face down, and crashes.
2. **The Morning After:** Her alarm goes off. She reaches for her phone to snooze it and sees an unread notification in her self-text thread timestamped at 3:14 AM.
3. **The Message:** 
   > `On the Rothschild case. Consider other factors. Maybe it is a vaccine reaction?`
4. **The Reaction:** 
   * **Confusion:** She stares at the screen. She remembers the Rothschild patient—a complex, inexplicable case that's been stalling out—but she has zero memory of typing that text at 3:00 AM.
   * **Rationalization:** She immediately diagnoses herself. *Too much wine. Sleep deprivation. Sleep-texting.* She brushes it off as a weird, subconscious brain dump while half-asleep.
5. **The Aftermath:** At the hospital later that morning, standing over Rothschild's chart, her logical brain can't ignore the suggestion. She reluctantly orders the panel—and it hits. 

### Why This Works
* **Grounded Realism:** Lauren’s first instinct isn't "time travel"; it's "I'm overworked and sleep-walking."
* **Medical Authenticity:** It showcases her analytical mind immediately trying to find a physiological cause for her own behavior.
