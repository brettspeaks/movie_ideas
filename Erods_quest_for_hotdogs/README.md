### This is the Cronicles of Erod: And Hotdogs

This book will lead Eron (and family) and through time and space from a 15000 year old living and thriving civilization with "Eron", a well studied engieer at heart with an ear for linguistics, guided my his magical "nuts" that allows him to warp space and time in an effort to save his civilization from doom, but also on the journey to find his way back 2026 where it all begins to eat hotdogs. 

This is his story.
