# Book 3: The Chronicles of Eron – The Uncalibrated Jumps

**Co-created by:** Bradley & Brett Speaks  
**Status:** In Outline / Early Draft  

---

## 📌 Logline
With Eron incapacitated, 8-year-old Cattie grabs the Seed-Resonance Orb staff and recruits her cautious, risk-averse 10-year-old brother Bradley on a high-stakes mission to save their community. Not understanding how the physical "nuts" calibrate spatial alignment, Cattie sends them on a chaotic spree of unpredictable time jumps across history while Bradley relies on his root-language instincts to keep them afloat.

---

## 🎯 Core Themes & Character Arcs
* **The Inverse Sibling Dynamic:** Cattie's action-oriented, button-pushing energy vs. Bradley's cautious, risk-averse grounding force.
* **The Linguistic Survivalist:** Bradley steps into his core talent—decoding root words and context on the fly ("I don't know much of any language, but I can find a bathroom anywhere").
* **Unpredictable Navigation:** Travelling without Eron's precision creates intense natural tension, as every jump is an uncalibrated gamble across time and geography.

---

## 🗺️ Chapter Breakdown

### **Chapter 1: The Incapacitated Engineer**
* **Setting:** Eron’s home village in the Pacific Scablands.
* **Key Events:**
  * An unexpected crisis threatens the village while Eron is out of commission.
  * Cattie decides waiting around isn't an option and grabs the staff.
  * She drags a hesitant 10-year-old Bradley along, convincing him that "it can't be that hard to steer."

### **Chapter 2: The First Wrong Turn**
* **Setting:** An unfamiliar historical epoch (uncalibrated jump).
* **Key Events:**
  * The pair land in a chaotic environment due to Cattie misinterpreting the staff's orientation.
  * Bradley gets his first real trial by fire: using root words, tone, and body language to navigate local authorities and secure safety for Cattie.

### **Chapter 3: Trial by Error**
* **Setting:** A series of rapid, erratic jumps across distinct historical eras.
* **Key Events:**
  * Cattie attempts physical adjustments to the "nuts" while mid-jump, leading to close calls.
  * Bradley becomes the master negotiator, sourcing supplies and finding shelter across totally different cultural contexts.

### **Chapter 4: The Unresolved Threat**
* **Setting:** An interim epoch while tracking the missing components to save home.
* **Key Events:**
  * The duo learns to operate as a tightly knit team despite their opposite approaches to danger.
  * Sets the stage for their arrival in post-occupation Korea in Book 4.
