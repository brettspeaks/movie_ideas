## 📌 Logline
When a deadly, mysterious avian flu strikes Eron’s home village in the pre-ice-age Pacific Scablands, he must embark on a cross-era journey to find a cure. Joined by his headstrong, fearless 8-year-old granddaughter Cattie, Eron pairs ancient logic with Cattie's boundary-pushing energy to face an invisible biological threat.

---

## 🎯 Core Themes & Character Arcs
* **Intergenerational Dynamic:** Eron’s calculated engineering logic vs. Cattie’s raw intuition and zero patience for "adult overthinking."
* **Biological vs. Mechanical Threats:** Eron shifts from solving mechanical/time-travel puzzles to fighting an unseen ecological and viral enemy to protect his lineage.
* **Ancient Technology in a Kid's Hands:** Cattie begins observing the Seed-Resonance Orb ("nuts"), treating her grandfather's time-travel tech as a fascinating toy rather than a dangerous artifact.

---

## 🗺️ Chapter Breakdown

### **Chapter 1: The Outbreak in the Scablands**
* **Setting:** Eron’s home village (15,000 years ago).
* **Key Events:**
  * A sudden avian flu outbreak threatens the community and Eron's family.
  * Eron realizes his local remedies and pre-ice-age tech aren't enough to isolate the strain.
  * Cattie refuses to stay behind, forcing Eron to take her along as his unlikely apprentice.

### **Chapter 2: The Medieval Aviary**
* **Setting:** Medieval Europe / Silk Road trade center during an avian migration event.
* **Key Events:**
  * Eron and Cattie jump to an era with documented historical bird handler techniques.
  * Cattie's fearless nature allows her to bond with local bird keepers while Eron decodes root words to gather ecological data on viral spread.

### **Chapter 3: The Botanical Expedition**
* **Setting:** An uncolonized, pristine ecological zone in ancient history.
* **Key Events:**
  * Searching for natural antiprotozoal and antiviral plant strains that went extinct in their home era.
  * Cattie pushes boundaries, testing unknown flora and forcing Eron to adapt quickly to her chaotic problem-solving style.

### **Chapter 4: The Cure and the Tease**
* **Setting:** Return to the Scablands (15,000 years ago).
* **Key Events:**
  * Armed with historical plant strains and cross-era ecological knowledge, Eron synthesizes a treatment and saves the village.
  * Cattie becomes deeply fascinated by the physical calibration "nuts" on Eron's staff, setting up her chaotic control of the staff in Book 3.
