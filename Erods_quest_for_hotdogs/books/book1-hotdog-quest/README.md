# Book 1: The Chronicles of Eron – The Hot Dog Quest

**Co-created by:** Bradley & Brett Speaks  
**Status:** In Outline / Early Draft  

---

## 📌 Logline
An ancient engineer from an advanced pre-ice-age civilization accidentally triggers a time-travel artifact. Stranded across history with a dying biological power source, his ultimate quest is to master the machine, secure the greatest future culinary achievement ($1.50 CostCan hot dog combo), and return to save his village from a catastrophic ice dam.

---

## 🎯 Core Themes & Character Arcs
* **Eron’s Journey:** Eron relies on raw engineering intuition, root-word linguistic decoding, and universal human gestures to navigate unknown eras.
* **Technology Shock:** Advanced ancient technology in Eron’s era is organic and biological, contrasting with modern electronics and ancient mechanical monuments.
* **The Universal Goal:** Pure survival paired with an unwavering dedication to the $1.50 hot dog combo ("The hot dog is NOT a lie").

---

## 🗺️ Chapter Breakdown

### **Chapter 1: The CostCan Incident (The Origin)**
* **Setting:** A modern-day CostCan parking lot and store.
* **Key Events:**
  * Eron materializes and mistakes gas pumps/EV chargers for "metal beasts attaching to hoses."
  * A cart wrangler confronts him, but **The CostCan Savior** (a single mother of three) shields him by claiming "he's with me."
  * Eron encounters modern culture shocks: collared shirts ("unnecessary neck flaps") and infinite sample stations.
  * In electronics, a tech-savvy girl inadvertently demonstrates wireless charging using a phone pad, causing Eron's orb to throb and absorb power.
  * Eron tries to buy hot dogs with a gifted plastic card and a "wrist-beep" gesture. The overloaded staff wobbles and teleports him mid-transaction.

### **Chapter 2: The Cradle of Civilization (The Megalithic Charger)**
* **Setting:** Bronze Age Egypt / Mesopotamia.
* **Key Events:**
  * Eron lands in a desert society that seems ancient to us, but primitive to him.
  * He attempts to buy bread using his CostCan gift card and a vocal *"Beep!"*, causing mass confusion.
  * **The Egyptian Guide** takes pity on him, finding his confusion charming, and guides him through the market.
  * Eron realizes regional monuments (pyramids/ziggurats) are massive wireless earth-energy chargers and uses them to power the staff for his next jump.

### **Chapter 3: The Chicago Bootlegger (1940s)**
* **Setting:** 1940s Chicago prohibition era.
* **Key Events:**
  * Eron lands in the middle of a bootlegging operation and is mistaken by a **Chicago Bootlegger** for a heavy-hitting foreign mercenary.
  * Roped into a heist, Eron gains access to high-voltage industrial power lines to charge his staff.
  * Picks up his favorite signature catchphrase: *"Give me a smoke and ahh, see?"*

### **Chapter 4: The Scablands Return**
* **Setting:** Eron’s home era (Pacific Scablands, 15,000 years ago).
* **Key Events:**
  * Supercharged and finally understanding how physical orientation steers the "nuts," Eron makes a final jump back to his timeline.
  * Armed with future insights and a hoard of hot dogs, he prevents the ice dam collapse and saves his village, setting the foundation for Book 2.
