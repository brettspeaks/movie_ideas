# Characters

## 👥 Primary Character Profiles

### Eron (The Pioneer)
* **Archetype:** Brilliantly practical ancient engineer; pragmatic, observation-driven, and stoic with a dry sense of humor.
* **Role:** Starts as a solo survivalist running on trial and error; eventually learns to trust Bradley's non-linear, multi-node temporal tactics.

### Bradley (The Grandmaster)
* **Archetype:** 10-year-old tactical visionary; analytical, risk-averse, and structurally focused.
* **Playstyle:** Views temporal mechanics like asymmetric chess. Prefers "structural mis-alignment" and seemingly silly pawn moves ($A \rightarrow B \rightarrow [C+D+E]$) to create unblockable chain reactions.
* **Key Trait:** Decodes the staff's root-language glyphs faster than anyone, turning chaotic temporal drift into a deterministic, step-by-step algorithm.

### Cattie (The Wildcard)
* **Archetype:** Fearless, instinct-driven action hero; resilient, chaos-tolerant, and fiercely loyal.
* **Playstyle:** Brute-forces her way through obstacles through sheer grit, whether surviving weeks alone in future culture shocks or wielding the staff with raw, uncalibrated power.

## 👥 Side Characters & Historical Encounter Profiles

### 🛒 The CostCan Savior (Modern Era)
* **Profile:** A hardworking, empathetic single mother of three kids.
* **Role:** Completely overwhelmed by her own grocery run, she spots Eron being harassed by a store employee. Guided by pure kindness, she steps in with a casual *"He's with me,"* shielding him from security and introducing him to future electronics and the food court.

### 🏺 The Egyptian Guide (Bronze Age)
* **Profile:** A sharp, playful local woman who finds Eron’s utter confusion and strange Atlantean features charming and "cute."
* **Role:** While Eron attempts to decode root words to find food, she takes pity on him. She guides him through the desert city and leads him straight to the regional "pyramids," unwittingly helping him find his massive wireless power source.

### 🕴️ The Chicago Bootlegger (1940s)
* **Profile:** A fast-talking, sharp-dressed mobster entangled in the city's illegal liquor distribution network.
* **Role:** He mistakes Eron for a heavy-hitting, eccentric foreign mercenary from a rival syndicate. He ropes Eron into a dangerous distribution run, inadvertently giving Eron access to the city's high-voltage industrial power lines and teaching him his favorite new catchphrase: *"Give me a smoke and ahh, see?"*
