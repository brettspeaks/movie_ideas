# Scene: The Dry Turkey

INT. CLOSTER COMMUNITY CENTER - DAY (DECEMBER 2016)

Fluorescent lights buzz over long folding tables arranged in parallel lines. Holiday tinsel mocks the atmospheric dread. 

MOM (40s, fierce, rural Kansas grit) sits rigidly at Table A. Opposite her at Table B is BIOLOGICAL MOM (50s), watching like a ghost who stepped out of the past. 

Between them sits the wreckage of the holiday.

ELDEST SISTER (24) slumps in a folding chair at the end of Table A. Her eyes are bloodshot and glassy. A plate of untouched, pale holiday food sits in front of her. She looks at the food, then across the narrow divide at Biological Mom, then back to her mother.

ELDEST SISTER
(Loudly, dripping with irony)
Welp. Better get some Scotch for this fucking dry turkey. This will be fun.

MOM
(Sotto, teeth gritted)
Put the cup down and eat your dinner. Right now.

ELDEST SISTER
(Slurring, ignoring her)
Why? We're celebrating, right? A family reunion. We survived Troy, Kansas. We survived the cornfields. Let's toast to the biological miracle across the aisle.

BIOLOGICAL MOM
(Quietly, observing)
She's hurting. 

MOM
(Snapping at Biological Mom)
Do not weigh in on my daughter. 

MOM (CONT'D)
(Turning furiously to Eldest Sister)
You are thirty minutes away from making a scene in front of this entire town. Your sisters worked their asses off to get through college, and you are sitting here drowning yourself because Sarah took the kid and left. 

ELDEST SISTER
(Voice cracking, rising)
She didn't just leave, Mom! She's *gone*. My entire life just walked out the door, and I'm supposed to sit here and play nice for her? For the woman who gave them away? I spent ten years covering for those two! Ten years making sure nobody in Troy saw them lift a tractor or burn a barn down!

Down the table, the TWINS sit side-by-side—a study in absolute opposites. 

TWIN 1 (The Straight-A Anchor) sits perfectly straight, staring at her plate, mortified, trying to maintain the family's "normal" facade. 

TWIN 2 (The Fluent Hedonist) has her chair tilted back on two legs. She looks slightly high, completely unbothered by the screaming, and casually reaches across the table to steal a roll from Biological Mom's side. She winks at her.

TWIN 2
Don't mind her. She's just mad she's the only one of us who can't handle her liquor.

MOM
(Pounding the table)
Shut up, both of you! This day is not about your marriage, and it is not about your vices. It is about keeping this family anchored. 

Eldest Sister looks at her Mom, a bitter, drunken realization hitting her. She stands up, unsteady on her feet.

ELDEST SISTER
It's never about me. I was just the watchdog. 

She leaves the plastic cup on the table and stumbles toward the double doors of the community center.

> **CRITIQUE & PROMPT NOTES:**
> * **The Scotch Line:** Starting the scene with her complaining about the "fucking dry turkey" sets the exact tone of aggressive, defensive misery. It lets the reader know immediately that she has entirely given up on being the mature, protective older sister today.
> * **The Core Conflict:** The mom trying to enforce traditional family milestone respectability ("eat your dinner") while the eldest sister's entire life is in ashes creates a brutal, uncomfortably realistic emotional disconnect.
