# Until Death Do Us Pageant

**Working Title Options:**
* *Until Death Do Us Pageant*
* *Black Tie & Prairie*
* *Coronation in Osage County*
* *Stiff Collars*

**Logline:** A pageant-obsessed mother’s attempt to throw a high-society black-tie gala in Ponca City, Oklahoma for her daughter’s wedding turns deadly when the groom’s ex-wife is murdered. The mother must use her pageant training to hide the body and save the ceremony, completely unaware she is being framed by the groom.

**Setting:** A grand, 1920s oil-baron estate mansion in Ponca City, Oklahoma.

---

## Core Characters

* **The Mother of the Bride (The Director):** Raised in northern Oklahoma pageant circuits. Fixated on perfection and visual control. Treats her daughter's wedding as her own long-denied coronation.
* **The Bride (The Reluctant Star):** Wants a simple life; caught between pleasing her mother and sensing the artificiality of the entire event.
* **The Groom / Second Husband (The Architect):** Previously married, calm, and calculated. Uses the mother’s pageant-bred obsession with optics to execute a murder and pin it on her.
* **The Ex-Wife (The Catalyst):** Uninvited guest who leverages her status as mother to the groom's son to crash staged photo ops and shatter the mother's fragile aesthetic.
* **The Distant Cousin (The Narrator/Observer):** Working-class relative sitting at the back table in an ill-fitting rental tux, serving as the grounded, sarcastic entry point for the story.

---

## Key Plot & Narrative Mechanics

* **The Cultural Friction:** Blue-collar farmers, ranchers, and pipe-fitters forced into strict black-tie attire in a gilded mansion, creating immediate social tension.
* **The Intrusion:** The ex-wife crashes the staged photo ops, threatening to expose financial or personal secrets that would ruin the groom's fresh start.
* **The Crime & Frame-Job:** The groom quietly kills his ex-wife in a side parlor and plants subtle evidence pointing directly to the mother of the bride (fabric from her dress, lipstick, vanity tools).
* **The Stage Manager Mindset:** Upon finding the body, the mother doesn't call the police—her pageant instincts kick in (*"The show must go on"*). She hides the body, wipes down the scene, and micro-manages the reception to preserve the gala's visual aesthetic.
* **The Trap Closes:** The mother’s frantic, secretive behavior makes her look undeniably guilty to the guests, the bride, and the law, while the groom plays the stoic, grieving husband.
