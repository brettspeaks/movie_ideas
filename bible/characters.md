# Squad Manifest: [Working Title]
**Genre:** Action / Urban Fantasy / Episodic
**Core Rules:** No time travel, high energy, fast pacing, TMNT-style group dynamics.

## 1. Kate
* **Role:** The Leader / Front & Center (The "Dad")
* **Age:** 8
* **K-Pop Specialty:** Leader & Main Vocal
* **Combat Style:** Tactical Command / Precision Strike
* **Personality & Vibe:** Runs the show, calls the high-stakes plays under pressure, and tries to keep the team on schedule. Owns the stage and the battlefield with total authority.

---

## 2. Anna Kate
* **Role:** The Tactical Anchor / Quiet Brain (The "Mom")
* **Age:** 8
* **K-Pop Specialty:** Visual & Frequency Synthesizer / Tech
* **Combat Style:** Grid Calculation, Trap Setting, & Exit Vectors
* **Personality & Vibe:** Calm, collected, and hyper-observant. While Kate sets the vision, Anna Kate does the behind-the-scenes math to ensure everyone survives the rift.

---

## 3. Audry
* **Role:** The Heavy Hitter / Executioner (The Middle Child)
* **Age:** 6
* **K-Pop Specialty:** Main Dancer & Martial Choreography
* **Combat Style:** High-Impact Heavy Combat / Line Breaker
* **Personality & Vibe:** Zero drama, zero nonsense. Wants to skip the talk, smash through the demon defenses, and get the job done so everyone can move on.

---

## 4. Cooper
* **Role:** The Wild Energy / Chaotic Element (The Youngest)
* **Age:** 5
* **K-Pop Specialty:** Hype / Main Rapper
* **Combat Style:** Unpredictable Destruction / Demolitions
* **Personality & Vibe:** A total tyrant of pure chaos. Operates on his own internal logic, breaks things just to watch them explode, and keeps morale high through sheer unfiltered momentum.
